# Zoosh brand: design note

Owner: Riaan van Dyk, Blugecko Applications. Original artwork for the free open-source integration; no Home Assistant artwork or third-party marks.

## Concept
"Zoosh" is the sound of a quick sweep. The mark is a translucent frosted-glass tile with one finely tapered, curved light-sweep blade (orange-red glowing through the glass) and a thin trailing line that suggests speed. No text in the icon.

## Construction (bottom to top)
1. Drop shadow (blurred rounded rect).
2. Glass body: 416 x 416 rounded square (radius 112) at 48,40 in a 512 canvas, translucent gradient.
3. Frost overlay and diagonal sheen, clipped to the tile.
4. Soft inner glow (blurred warm-white inner stroke) and orange bloom behind the blade.
5. Blade: one path, pointed at both ends, gradient tail (translucent) to head (warm white); blurred inner highlight line down its spine.
6. Trailing speed line, low opacity, round caps.
7. Edge: hairline plus gradient rim.
Wordmark: NO FONT, no text elements, no traced or copied typeface. Style is inspired by tall monoline geometric display type, but all letterforms are original: round caps and joins, one stroke weight, pill-shaped o.

### A. Primary: lowercase "zoosh" (brand/logo*.png, logo.svg, dark_logo.svg; canvas 502 x 224)
Drawn in a 227-wide row, then scaled 1.1 (about 101 tall, about 53 % of the icon height) and centred on the icon's visual centre. Stroke 11, letter box 36 wide, x-height 76 (y 20-96, the o's top and bottom), h ascender 92 (y 4-96).
Shared geometry: the o is a stadium with centreline x 5..31, y 25..91 and radius 13 (semicircle centres (18,38) and (18,78)).
- o, o: that stadium (rect 26 x 66, rx 13).
- h: stem at x 5 from y 9 to 91; the arch is the o's top semicircle (centre (18,38), radius 13, same stroke) from the stem to x 31, then a straight leg to the baseline. Arch top aligns exactly with the o's top.
- s: lower bowl is the o's bottom semicircle (r 13, centre (18,78), full o width). Upper bowl is a slightly smaller circle, r 12, centre (17.5,37), so it is 24 wide (7.7 % narrower than the o), its top still on the o's top (y 25), left edge at x 5.5 and right terminal pulled in to about x 29.5. The two circles are joined by their internal common tangent (a straight spine at about 38 degrees from vertical), so the outline is tangent-continuous. Terminals are the arcs cut at 30 degrees, round caps.
- z: flat top and bottom bars, straight diagonal; the bottom bar ends in a small return: "H26 Q31 91 31 85", an upturn of radius about 5 units. Orange-red #f23f0d.
- Letter origins (x): z 0, o 48, o 96, s 144, h 191; visual gaps about 11 (z to o), 11 (o to o and o to s) and 11 (s to h).
- Colours: z #f23f0d, "oosh" #1d2b4a (light logo) or #fff3ec (dark logo). Flat solid fills.

### B. Alternate: uppercase "ZOOSH" (branding-source/alt-uppercase/, canvas 540 x 224)
Row of 239 wide scaled 1.1, stroke 11, cap height 96 (about 1/9 of the height is stroke, letter width 40, ratio about 2.4). Z flat bars with a straight diagonal; O stadium 29 x 85, radius 14.5; S conventional monoline S built from the same two-bowl cubic path; H two verticals and a centred crossbar. Origins (x): Z 0, O 50, O 99, S 148, H 199 (gaps 9-11, a little wider where the letters are straight). Same colours as A.

## Files
SVGs: icon.svg, logo.svg (A), dark_icon.svg, dark_logo.svg (A), alt-uppercase/logo.svg and dark_logo.svg (B). PNGs: brand/ holds icon 256/512 and logo A at 574x256 and 1148x512 (plus dark_ copies); alt-uppercase/ holds logo B PNGs at 617x256 and 1234x512. PREVIEW-wordmarks.png compares A and B on white and #1c1c1e. Helper scripts: generate_svgs.py, render_png.sh, optimise_png.py.

## Usage rules
- Minimum: icon 32 px (checked), logo 64 px tall legible (checked), 120 px wide minimum.
- Clear space: 1/4 icon width on every side.
- Do: use dark_ files on dark backgrounds; keep transparency.
- Don't: recolour, stretch, rotate, add extra shadows, put the light logo on dark backgrounds, or add text in the icon.

## Regenerate
No rsvg/ImageMagick/Inkscape installed; PNGs are rendered by headless Chrome, then size-optimised with the stdlib script.

    python3 generate_svgs.py .          # rewrites all SVGs incl. alt-uppercase/
    export RENDER_TMP=/some/scratch/dir
    ./render_png.sh "$PWD/logo.svg" 574 256  OUT/logo.png      # @2x: 1148 512
    ./render_png.sh "$PWD/alt-uppercase/logo.svg" 617 256 OUT/logo.png   # @2x: 1234 512
    ./render_png.sh "$PWD/icon.svg" 256 256 OUT/icon.png       # @2x: 512 512
    python3 -I optimise_png.py 4 OUT/logo.png                  # repeat per file (4 = colour step; lossy but visually clean)

render_png.sh runs Chrome --headless=new --disable-gpu --screenshot=OUT --default-background-color=00000000 --window-size=W,H --hide-scrollbars --force-device-scale-factor=1 on a temp HTML page (in RENDER_TMP) that embeds the SVG. Same commands for the dark_ files. Verify with `sips -g pixelWidth -g pixelHeight -g hasAlpha`.
